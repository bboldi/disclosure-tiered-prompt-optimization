# Expanded source-review closeout

The complete 30,335-candidate screen found 824 numeric NVD/CNA agreements, including explicit affected and unaffected status support. The final source-only exclusions leave **661 admissible CVEs**. The [version-03 data card](../benchmark-20260912-03/DATA_CARD.md) documents the generated cohort and its limited applicability claim.

The original nine-case audit remains in `benchmark-20260912-01/SOURCE_REVIEW.md`. This expanded review selected two cases per publication-year × predicate-shape cell by a fixed SHA-256 order. It retains 26 reviewed records: 18 surviving cases and eight exclusions, replenishing excluded sample positions in that same order. These are assistant reviews, not independent human assessments or a population error-rate estimate. Individual rationales, hashes and final sample membership are in `source-review.json`.

The review exposed scope information absent from structured applicability fields. OpenSSL's ARM-specific AES-XTS defect is unsuitable for inventories without architecture. Its [official advisory](https://mta.openssl.org/pipermail/openssl-announce/2023-April/000257.html) corroborates that restriction; the old linked text URL returned 404, and both the failure and successful replacement acquisition are retained. A conservative text-cue screen was then applied across all 824 candidates, flagging 108. These cues deliberately accept false exclusions and do not prove every implicit prerequisite is absent from surviving records.

The inspected [IBM bulletin](https://www.ibm.com/support/pages/node/7148015) corroborates its listed affected versions but qualifies what can be inferred about unlisted unsupported releases. We conservatively exclude all 58 IBM candidates rather than use blanket unaffected defaults for arbitrary negatives. Five overlap the text-cue exclusions. This policy does not assert that their positive labels are wrong.

For CVE-2024-1297, the [discoverer's Loomio advisory](https://fluidattacks.com/advisories/stones) identifies 2.22.1, whereas the pinned CNA/NVD predicate specifies only 2.22.0; the page also contains an inconsistent full-name version. That case is quarantined. CVE-2023-45582 has an internal FortiMail discrepancy: text excludes 6.4.8, while structured fields include it and an additional branch. It is also quarantined without synthesizing a preferred range.

The resulting union is 163 exclusions from the numeric-agreement cohort: 108 text cues + 58 IBM cases − 5 overlap + 2 additional contradictions. Together with the original five disputes, the frozen review has 168 quarantine entries. The [Os Commerce discoverer advisory](https://fluidattacks.com/advisories/bts) independently lists the selected 4.12.56860 version; negative claims still rely on explicit CNA status rather than exhaustive testing of other releases.

Full pinned CNA records are in `../cna-corroboration-20260912-02/records/`. `vendor-references/` retains downloaded supporting pages and retrieval failures; `textual-review-evidence.json`, `retained-review-evidence.json` and `replacement-review/` retain the reviewed source fields. `textual-scope-and-vendor-audit.json` covers every numeric candidate. `admission-closeout.json` and `source-review.json` bind final decisions, source evidence and policy to the screened population.

The pilot admission gate validates the source hashes, selected review dispositions, text-cue quarantine, preserved partitions and complete generated CNA decision audit. No Optimizer or model inference supplied gold labels, selected the cohort, or repaired conflicting public sources.
